About Me

Strategic Design Leadership

14+ years transforming complex business challenges into clear, compelling visual systems that drive measurable results.

I'm a Senior Graphic Designer and Art Director based in Beirut, Lebanon, with a Master's degree in Graphic Design from Oklahoma City University.

Currently leading a five-person creative team at GCEL MEA, I specialize in translating complex commerce, technology, and economic content into clear visual systems. My work spans brand identity, corporate communications, presentation design, advertising campaigns, and digital experiences.

I'm passionate about creating visual narratives that clarify complexity and build confidence. Every project is an opportunity to combine strategic thinking with creative excellence.
